Here's the progress report:

* The game engine is 100% done, as is the infrastructure to port it to the Google Play Store (nothing on iOS yet, though)

* All the rooms are now in place (there are 52 total)

* All of the enemies are also done (there are 27 kinds total)

* Acts 1, 2, and 3 of the game are done, with Act 1 having recieved major updates to make it more playable. Only Act 4 remains.

* 3 of the 5 boss fights are done

What's left:

* Some level design for Act 4

* 2 boss fights

* Lots of polish, especially art and sound


The next version (0.8) will be a complete game, and that will be the first version which will be available to the public as an open beta.
